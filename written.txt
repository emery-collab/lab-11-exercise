The batched and non-batched implementations achieved relatively similar average FPS in my testing. 
Although batching reduces the number of draw calls by combining sprites into a single rendering operation, 
this did not produce a significant performance improvement in my test. This suggests that draw-call 
overhead was not a major performance bottleneck for the workload being tested. Overall, I would have expected
the implementation with batching to have superior fps, due to less draw calls being required, and the difference
seen here is slight, and almost minimal.

One way to implement depth is to assign different Z coordinates to sprites in clip space and enable OpenGL's 
depth testing using glEnable(GL_DEPTH_TEST). The rasterization pipeline can then use the depth buffer to 
automatically determine which sprites should appear in front of others, eliminating the need to manually sort 
sprites by depth on the CPU.
